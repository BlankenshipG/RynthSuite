# RynthAi 0.5.17-legacy-ui: item info line matches the ACECustom appraisal

Date: 2026-10-05
Branch: `feat/t11-loot-support` (uncommitted, on top of 0.5.16)

## Problem

The item info line (`/ra iteminfo`, ID on click) printed values that disagreed with the
appraisal panel. Example, a T11 Fire Staff:

```
Flaming Quarter Staff (Fire Staff), Craft 9
  FireRend, CritMult (2.66), 1,789.68-2069, 0.14v, 20%a, 20%md
  [CD 24]
```

The appraisal panel shows Damage 1790 - 2069, Crushing Blow 3.66x, Fire Rending +176%,
Attack +20%, Melee Defense +20.0%, Weapon Grade S (100%) and "Wield requires: 2,000 Item
Augmentations".

## Fixes (`ItemInfo/MagItemDescriber.cs`)

- **Damage range:** the low end is rounded to a whole number like the client
  (`2069 * (1 - 0.135)` gives 1790), with no group separator or decimals. Variance now shows
  three places (`0.135v`), so values ending in 5 are no longer rounded off.
- **Attack and defense bonuses** are signed like the appraisal panel (`+20%a`, `+20%md`).
  Penalties keep their `-`. A bonus that rounds to 0 is no longer printed.
- **Crushing Blow:** shows the multiplier a crit actually deals, 1 + CriticalMultiplier
  (`Crushing Blow 3.66x`), instead of the raw `CritMult (2.66)`. As on the server, a stored 1.0
  (a normal 2x crit) is not shown.
- **Biting Strike:** shown as a crit chance (`Biting Strike 15%`) instead of `CritFreq (0.15)`.
- **Imbue strengths** are read from the "Property Details:" text: `FireRend +176%`,
  `AR 30.0%`, `CS +12.5%`, `CB 2.5x`. Rendings are matched by damage keyword, and a culture
  space before `%` is accepted. Items without detail text keep the bare Mag labels (`CS AR`).
- **T11 data from the item text:**
  - weapon grade on the identity line (`Grade S (100%)`, under the Damage field setting);
  - item-aug, Triune Weave and charm wield gates on the requirements line
    (`Wield 2,000 Item Augs`, under the Wield setting);
  - Cast on Strike procs (`Proc Force Arc 13%`, under the Imbues setting);
  - zone modifiers on the ratings line (`Mods: Damage Resist +6, Max Health +100`, under the
    Ratings setting).
- A raw `WieldRequirement.Int64Stat` (13) requirement no longer prints as `Skill 9008 2500`.

Result for the staff above:

```
Flaming Quarter Staff (Fire Staff), Grade S (100%), Craft 9
  FireRend +176%, Crushing Blow 3.66x, 1790-2069, 0.135v, +20%a, +20%md
  [CD 24]  Wield 2,000 Item Augs
```

## Tests

`Tools/RynthCore.RynthAiHostTests/Tests/MagItemDescriberTests.cs` adds 5 tests:
- the staff from the report, line for line;
- Biting Strike and a defense penalty;
- bare and detailed imbue labels;
- Int64 wield requirement hidden;
- T11 armor modifiers and Triune gate.

All 5 pass. In the full host run, the only failure is the dungeon map bake check, which reads
the real dat files and is unrelated to this change.
