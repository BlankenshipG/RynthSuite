# 2026-11-08 — Loot checklist: "Plus a rend" (an imbue set and any rend)

Silentkelpie, with a Copper Frost Knife (Armor Rending, Cold Rending, Biting Strike): "armor rend is one imbue, and
then a elemental rend also can be on a item ... it should be able to find items with armor rend + bs + cs, but also
with any elemental on it. right now it will pickup items that match any one of them."

- The checklist had one Imbue row and one comparison. "has any" of Armor Rending + Any elemental rend took any of
  them; "has all" needed every element at once. There was no way to say "Armor Rending, and a rend of any element".
- New row under Item, "Plus a rend", right after Imbue: "has any" of the rends ("Any elemental rend", "Any physical
  rend", "Any magical-element rend", or one rend). It is its own test, besides the Imbue row's. Example: Imbue
  "has all" Armor Rending + Critical Strike, with "Plus a rend: Any elemental rend". The rule is one Has Flag each
  for Armor Rending and Critical Strike, plus one Has Flag of all the rends, and it takes an item with Armor
  Rending, Critical Strike and a rend of any element. Biting Strike and Crushing Blow are rows of their own (Weapon
  cards) and add their tests as before.
- The row lists the rends the ticked class can carry. An "any" pick stays while two or more of its rends fit, so
  "Any elemental rend" on a sword still works, since Nether never turns up there. Armor, clothing and jewelry
  carry no rends, so they have no row.
- Editing a rule reads a Has Flag of several rends on the imbue back into "Plus a rend". This happens when the
  Imbue row has its own test, or the rule tests the imbue more than once. Nothing is left over "as written".
- An item's own checklist (Add from an item, Filter inventory) starts an item with two or more imbues at
  "has all" of them, not "has any" (which took an item with any one of them).
- Same rows in the Loot Editor's Add rule and edit, Filter inventory and Global Inventory.
