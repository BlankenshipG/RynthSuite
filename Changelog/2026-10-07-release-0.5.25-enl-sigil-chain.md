# RynthAi 0.5.25-legacy-ui - Enlightenment calculator with the 301-325 sigil craft chain

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Previous release:** 0.5.24-legacy-ui
**Branch:** `main`
**Pairs with:** RynthCore engine 2026.10.5.19, which draws the new tables in its Skills panel
**Augments** tab. With an older engine the plan lines and auto-enlighten still work; the per-level,
material summary and sigil chain tables are not drawn.

## Why

Enlightenment levels 301-325 cost Enlightenment Sigils. Each target level T needs T - 5 sigils and
T x 2B luminance, which is ACECustom's tier 5 rate; RynthAi's rates already matched it. On
InfiniteLeaftide, sigils are crafted one-for-one:

- Enlightened Coin + Ancient Yalani Trinket = Ascension Coin
- Life Splitter + Plant = Lifebloom Resin
- Lifebloom Resin + Necrolytic Extract = Lifeblood Elixir
- Lifeblood Elixir + Ascension Coin = Enlightenment Sigil

Until now the planner showed only "N sigils". It now breaks that number down the chain and adds
the rest of UB-IT's Enlighten tab, matching UB-IT 2.0.1281.

## Chain math (new `IltHub/IltSigilChain.cs`)

- `Shortfall(sigils, stock)` uses stock from the top of the chain down: finished sigils first, then
  Ascension Coins and Elixirs, then Resins, then raw items. It returns the crafts left
  (Ascension Coins, Elixirs, Resins) and the raw items still to get (e-coins, trinkets, extracts,
  splitters, plants).
- `Fulfillable(stock)` counts how many sigils you can make: finished sigils plus the scarcer of the
  two craft paths (Ascension Coins, Elixirs).
- `EcoinDemand` splits the e-coin pool. Ascension Coin crafts take coins first, then token
  conversion at the shop price.
- The chain items have no WCIDs yet, so they are counted by name. Singular and plural stack names
  both match (case ignored). "Plant" must be the whole name.

## Snapshot (`IltProgression.AppendEnlJson`)

- Chain stock is read in `RefreshFacts`. E-coins count the bank plus the pack.
- New `enl` fields:
  - `levels`: one row per target level, up to 100: level, material, count, luminance, and ok. A row
    is ok while stock lasts, taking the levels in order; sigils count the craft chain.
  - `materials`: still need and have for luminance and each material in the plan. The Sigils row is
    ok when the chain covers the shortfall.
  - `chain` and `chainNote`: crafts left and raw items for the 301-325 band (empty otherwise).
  - `ecoins` and `ecoinsLum`: the e-coins needed for Ascension Coins plus tokens, and the
    luminance to buy them at Lum per e-coin.
- When sigils are the blocker, the message adds how many more the craft chain can make. Auto
  enlighten still counts finished sigils only, because the server consumes finished sigils.

## Tests (`Tools/RynthCore.RynthAiHostTests`, new `SigilChainTests.cs`)

- 301-325 rates (T - 5 sigils, T x 2B luminance, crests from 326) and `SigilsForRange` clamping.
- Shortfall with an empty pack, top-down use of partial stock, and finished sigils covering the
  demand.
- `Fulfillable` and the shared e-coin pool.
- Name matching: plural stacks, case and spaces, "Plant" exact, Lifebloom vs Lifeblood.
- Full run: 445 tests, 439 passed, 5 known failures. The one failure is the existing map-bake
  check, which depends on the local DAT files.
