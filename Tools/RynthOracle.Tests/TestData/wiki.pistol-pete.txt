== Discord Description ==
Pete wants trophies from the rarest beasts of Dereth.

== Overview ==
"Pistol" Pete in Candeth Keep collects trophies. Hunt each beast and bring him its trophy.

== Walkthrough & Notes ==
Talk to [["Pistol" Pete]] in Candeth Keep to get the list of trophies.

The Nephrite Golem roams the hills at 85.4N 6.4W. Kill it and loot its {{Itemlink|Nephrite Tooth}}.

Winston the Mattekar wanders near 62.8S, 97.9E. Kill him for {{Itemlink|Winston's Leg}}.

The Frost Wyrm sleeps in a cave at 62.7s 66.6W; the cave's exit is at 85.8s,78.8w. Kill it and take its {{Itemlink|Frozen Wyrm Scale}}.

Return to Pete with all three trophies.
